## How it works

In [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev), a video is a React component that receives the current frame number. Animation is just math on that number, and rendering opens each frame in a headless browser, captures it and encodes the result.

## Where it's strong

- **Repeatable style.** Change one brand file and every video follows.
- **Exact sync.** Tie an animation to the frame a word is spoken.
- **AI agents.** Scenes are text files, so an agent can write and fix them.
- **Data and UI.** Charts, mock apps and terminals stay crisp at any size.

## Where an editor is better

If your reel is mostly filmed footage cut by feel, a normal editor is faster. Remotion can use video clips, but it shines when the frame is built, not filmed.

## Licensing

Remotion isn't under a standard open-source licence. It's free for individuals and small teams, and companies with four or more people need a company licence. Check the [current licence](https://www.remotion.dev/license) before commercial use.

## What it doesn't give you

Remotion is the engine, not the look. Motion rules, components, voice, captions and review all sit on top. That layer is what [Abu Reel Kit](/how-it-works) provides.
