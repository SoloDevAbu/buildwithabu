## The working margins

These are the values built into Abu Reel Kit. They're approximate, so keep a little extra room.

| Edge | Keep clear | What sits there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top | 220 px | Reels title, camera icon |
| Bottom | 380 px | Username, caption, audio name |
| Right | 120 px | Like, comment, share and save buttons |
| Left | 60 px | Margin from the screen edge |

That leaves a safe box of 900 × 1320 px, starting 60 px from the left and 220 px from the top.

## The bottom is the biggest trap

A long caption pushes the overlay higher, so the bottom zone is the one to respect most. Put your main card in the upper-middle of the frame and captions near the top of the safe box.

## How to check a frame

Open a still at full size and draw the zones on top in a loud colour, then look for anything important under the red. Do it at several points in the video, because a card that fits at rest can slide under the buttons mid-animation.

In Abu Reel Kit, review rounds draw these zones on the frames automatically, and you can change the values in `src/layout.ts`.

## Do they apply to Shorts and TikTok?

Those apps have similar overlays in similar places, though the exact sizes differ. A layout that's safe on Reels is a sensible start, but check a real upload on each app.
