## Your three options

| Option | Needs internet | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) | Yes | Good default quality, including Indian English and Hindi |
| [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) | Only for the first download | Offline use |
| Your own recording | No | Personal brands |

## Make it sound like talking

Generated voices sound robotic most often because of pauses, not tone:

- **Speak each paragraph in one go.** Stitching separate sentences creates uneven gaps.
- **Cap the pauses.** Abu Reel Kit caps pauses at 0.18 seconds and adds longer ones only where the script asks for a beat.
- **Keep the pace up.** Its target is about four words a second.
- **Master the level.** It masters speech to -16 LUFS so the voice stays consistent between reels.

## Before you publish commercially

Terms differ by service and can change, so read the current terms for the voice you use. edge-tts is an unofficial client for a Microsoft service, not a licensed product. Kokoro-82M is released under Apache 2.0. If you're making work for a client, your own recording or a clearly licensed voice is the safest choice.
