## The failure to design against

Language models write smoothly whether or not a claim is true. Asking the agent to "be accurate" doesn't change that. A process does.

## Four rules

1. **Only opened sources count.** Search snippets don't. Keep a list of the pages actually read.
2. **Every number traces back.** If a number is on screen, it must appear in the research notes.
3. **No source, no claim.** Cut it, or say plainly that it's your opinion.
4. **A person approves.** The agent can verify that a source exists, but you decide whether it's any good.

## Make the check mechanical

Rules in a prompt get skipped. A script doesn't skip them. In Abu Reel Kit, `pnpm run facts` rejects any claim without an opened source, and any on-screen number that isn't in the research. The voice stage won't run until you have approved a script.

## Your part

No tool makes this automatic. Facts in your reels are your responsibility, so open the key sources before you say yes, especially for health, money or legal topics. Primary sources beat articles about them.
